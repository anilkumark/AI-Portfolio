# General Investment Risk Disclosure Statement

**Document ID:** RISK-DISC-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** July 2026
**Review Date:** October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This statement discloses the general risks associated with investing in the
asset classes offered through this platform. It is provided for client
education and must accompany any risk-based advice shared with a client.

## 2. Market Risk

All securities are subject to market risk — the possibility that the overall
market will decline, reducing the value of individual investments regardless
of company or issuer performance. Equities generally carry higher market risk
than fixed income or cash equivalents.

## 3. Concentration Risk

Concentration risk arises when a portfolio holds a disproportionately large
position in a single security, sector, or asset class. A single holding
exceeding roughly 10-15% of total portfolio value is generally considered a
concentration risk, since adverse performance in that holding has an outsized
effect on total portfolio value. Diversification across issuers, sectors, and
asset classes is the primary mitigant.

## 4. Liquidity Risk

Real estate and certain alternative investments may not be readily converted
to cash without a loss of value, particularly during market stress. Clients
with near-term liquidity needs should limit allocation to illiquid asset
classes accordingly.

## 5. Volatility and Risk Scoring

Portfolios are assigned a risk score reflecting the weighted volatility of
their holdings by asset class, combined with a concentration flag when any
single holding exceeds the concentration threshold in Section 3. Higher risk
scores indicate greater expected variability in portfolio value and are not,
by themselves, a recommendation to buy or sell.

## 6. Advisory Disclaimer

Any mitigation strategy suggested by this platform is a general educational
observation based on the client's current holdings, not personalized
investment advice, and must be reviewed by a qualified compliance reviewer
before being presented to a client as actionable guidance.
