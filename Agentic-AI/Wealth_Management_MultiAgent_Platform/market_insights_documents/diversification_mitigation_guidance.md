# Diversification and Concentration Risk Mitigation Guidance

**Document ID:** RISK-MIT-003
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** July 2026
**Review Date:** October 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document provides standard, pre-approved mitigation strategies that may
be referenced when a portfolio is flagged for high risk or concentration,
subject to compliance review before being shared with a client.

## 2. Rebalancing Toward Target Allocation

Portfolios drifting from their target allocation due to uneven asset
performance should be rebalanced back toward target weights on a regular
schedule (typically quarterly or semi-annually), rather than reactively
during periods of volatility.

## 3. Trimming Concentrated Positions

When a single holding exceeds the concentration threshold, a partial
reduction — reallocating the excess into other holdings or asset classes —
reduces single-issuer risk without requiring a full exit from the position.
A common target is capping any single holding at 10-15% of total portfolio
value.

## 4. Diversifying Across Asset Classes

Portfolios weighted heavily toward a single asset class benefit from
broader diversification across equities, fixed income, real estate, and
cash, since these asset classes do not typically move in lockstep.

## 5. Staged Implementation

Any mitigation strategy involving the sale of existing positions should be
implemented in stages rather than all at once, to reduce transaction-timing
risk and potential tax impact — this should be noted when presenting
mitigation suggestions to a client.
